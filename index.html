Privacy and limits

Image files are read locally by your browser. OCR is performed using Tesseract.js downloaded from a public CDN. Excel export uses SheetJS from a public CDN. No image upload or paid API is used by this app. This app does not send your contacts to a server.

For 500 photos, keep Chrome open and your phone charged. Process in batches of 50–100 if your phone has limited memory. Export regularly so you don't lose work if the tab closes.
